The degree of citation extends only to necessary information. In addition, if works are summarized for citation, works should be faithfully summarized.

②Please clearly separate the cited part.
Showing the cited part in brackets or square frames is the most desirable method. At very least, please do not cite works on this site in a manner that may lead to misunderstanding that they are works of users.

③Users should treat their works as “main” and citations as “subordinate.”

④Please specify the source according to one of four examples below.
Please place the source description as close as possible to the citation to clarify the connection between the citation and the source.

2．Links

Links to this site are welcomed. Advance contact is unnecessary. However, please follow the points below.

(1) Please clearly state that the link is to the Official Worldwide JLPT Website.
(2) Please do not set up a link that embeds this site in another website.
